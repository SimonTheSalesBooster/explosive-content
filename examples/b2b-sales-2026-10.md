## No proven demand (yet)

- **cost of inaction**, **stop being needy in sales**, **stop discounting B2B**: not one long-form video beat its channel's subscriber count.
- Weak: **how to raise prices B2B** (2.5x, a street-sales video) and **close bigger B2B deals** (2.1x).

## What it told us

1. **What buyers want explodes.** Leads, cold email, a pipeline, AI tools, closing. That's how a founder searches.
2. **What's actually missing doesn't get searched.** Nobody types "cost of inaction". So those ideas go *inside* the video as the method, never in the title.
3. **AI GTM and the AI sales stack** reach 10x to 18x. The category label is hot right now.

This is the order we record in. Your list will look different: run it on your own keywords.
